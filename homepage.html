Science Fiction Books

The forever War

The Forever War is a military science fiction novel by American author Joe Haldeman, telling the contemplative story about human soldiers fighting an interstellar war against an alien civilization known as the Taurans.

Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?

Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? is a 1968 dystopian science fiction novel by American writer Philip K. Dick. It is set in a post-apocalyptic San Francisco, where Earth's life has been greatly damaged by a nuclear global war.

The Man Who Fell to Earth

Thomas Newton arrives on earth to steal water for his own planet. He uses the advanced technology on his planet to make wealth on earth and fund the construction of a spaceship to make his way back.
